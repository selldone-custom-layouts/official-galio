# Galio Sundae — Selldone Layout

**Galio Sundae Patisserie** (package `galio-sundae-patisserie`, first version `1.0.0`) is the fourth Layout of the Selldone shop **Bakio** (shop 15740, xapi name `bakio`, `https://bakio.myselldone.com`): one pâtisserie at 1 The Embarcadero, San Francisco, selling individual pastries for courier delivery, worldwide shipping and pickup, cash on delivery, guest checkout. Single seller, English only, USD only. It is built for its own domain, **galio.selldone.shop**; the shop default (Layout 36, Bakini Noir) and domain 1247 are never changed by this Layout.

The design is mockup set **B, "Sundae"** (`../galio-design/home-b.html`, its source in `g/{core.css,b.css,home.js,icons.js,photos.js,theme-color.js}`, screenshots in `shots/`): a sorbet-coloured pastry parlour whose sections follow Qode's Glace ice-cream shop theme. Build brief: `../galio-design/BUILD-GALIO.md`. The inner pages' mockups (`../galio-design/<page>-b.html`) are drawn by another session (brief `BRIEF-GALIO-PAGES.md`) and are ported in phase 2.

It started from Bakini Noir 1.0.5 (`bakini-layout`, `git clone`, history kept), so every Selldone flow Noir solved is kept: the bag, checkout, address and map sheets, payment, order, account and sign-in, the blog, compare, the i18n runtime and `t()` with Arabic right to left, the region pill, the theme switcher row, fit-in-circle, the release scripts and the live checks.

## 1.0.3 — one colour family (14 November 2026)

**Why.** The owner looked at the live product page and found too many unrelated colours (lemon, strawberry, mint and sky bands, a cocoa rail, a peach, yellow or pink plate per collection, yellow stickers and dot) and no single theme colour; a palette only changed the buttons. Approved: "one colour family that follows the chosen theme". The spec is `galio-design/BUILD-ONE-FAMILY.md`, `BRIEF-ONE-FAMILY.md`, the mockups `home-one-*.html`, `product-one-*.html` (shots in `galio-design/shots/one/`) and their code `g/family.js` and `g/one.css`.

**How.** Every colour comes from one hue, the chosen palette's, plus white and the family's near-black.
- `components/galio/galio-family.ts` is `g/family.js` in TypeScript: from the palette's colour (the presets' seeds `FAMILY_SEEDS`, the visitor's Custom colour, or the merchant's `brandColor`) it builds the OKLCH family (keep the hue, cap the chroma) for light and dark, `--gl-fam-50/100/200/300`, `--gl-fam-ink-bg`, grounds, words, lines, the dark bars' words (`--gl-on-ink*`), the photo slots' ground, the scrim and the drawn map, and writes the current mode's values on `<html>`. `bootFamily()` runs in `storefront.ts` right after the theme switcher and follows every palette, mode and Custom change (a MutationObserver on `<html>` and a watch on the switcher's state); it also gives Vuetify's dark theme (Selldone's dialogs, menus, fields) the family's night.
- `style/galio/one.css` is generated from `g/one.css` by `scripts/galio-css.cjs` (which now maps `html[data-mode]`, "either mode", to `html:root`, as heavy as `html.gl-dark` and later, so the token map wins in both modes): the Cherry family as plain CSS for both modes (right on the first paint), Galio's tokens mapped onto the family (bands, plates, rail, top bar, footer, sticker, note, map, photo slots), the home's bands taking turns (collections `fam-100`, price list white, banner `fam-ink-bg`, best sellers `fam-100`, visit white, journal `fam-50`), every plate `fam-200`, white collection discs, the product's buy box `fam-100`, "Why you'll love it" `fam-50`, spec stripes `fam-50`.

## 1.0.4 — shapes, option "Awning" (14 November 2026)

**Why.** The owner found the shapes had no harmony: half-circles running down the rail, half-circles under the page title, sharp straight bars next to rounded cards and pill buttons ("like a card with sharp corners and a curved button"). Of two mockup options they picked **Awning**. The spec is `galio-design/BUILD-AWNING.md`, `BRIEF-SHAPES.md` (option B), the mockups `home-/product-/category-awning.html` (shots in `galio-design/shots/shapes/`), their CSS `g/shape-awning.css` and the audit `check/shape-check.cjs`. `g/shape.js` is mockup plumbing and was not ported, nor the mockup's note label.

**How.**
- **One radius scale everywhere:** circle 50% (plates, the sticker, icon discs, round buttons, swatches), pill 999px (buttons, tabs, chips, inputs, list rows), panel 28px (cards, panels, the hero, every photo, dialogs, drawers, sheets, the joins), inner 14px (photo labels, captions, text areas). `style/galio-shape.scss` sets the tokens (`--gl-r-panel/-inner/-pill/-circle`, and `--gl-r-s/-m/-hero/-tile` onto them) and the mockup's per-part choices; `scripts/galio-css.cjs` now puts every fixed px radius of the generated layers on the scale (20px and up → 28, under 20 → 14; a radius that is half its rule's height becomes a pill), and the same mapping was applied once to `style/galio.scss` and `style/galio-buy.scss` (the payment window, the address sheet, the order page, chat bubbles, skeletons). Selldone's and Vuetify's buttons are pills and icon buttons circles, at rest and focused.
- **Focus rings keep the control's shape:** pills stay pills, circles circles (the mockup's `:focus-visible` lists), inset as before.

## 1.0.5 — focus: no outline, one ring per control (14 November 2026)

**The owner's bug.** Clicking the Min/Max price box of the collection filter (live 1.0.4, the desktop panel and the phone sheet) showed three rings at once: a sharp rectangle around the bare input, a 14px ring around the whole label (over the word "Min") and the right one, the `.rb` pill. Causes: the base Layout's `.v-application :focus-visible { outline: 2px solid; outline-offset: 2px }` (`style/kalio-tokens.scss`), which outweighed core.css's `input:focus { outline: none }` and reached every focused element with no rule of its own; and the mockup's `label:focus-within` ring (`style/galio/core.css`), drawn on any label that wraps a field.

**Fix** (`style/galio-shape.scss`): no outline on anything focused, in Galio and in Selldone's and Vuetify's parts; a label that wraps a field never rings (the field, or its own wrapper such as `.rb` or `.sortsel`, carries the one ring on its own shape; checkboxes and switches ring on their own box); and anything outside Galio's markup with no ring of its own (a link, a button, a `[tabindex="0"]`) gets an inset ring on its own radius, so nothing lost its focus look.

**The check that missed it.** The ring sweep and the shape audit looked at pages at rest and at links and buttons focused from the keyboard, never at a clicked field. `scripts/shape-check.cjs` now clicks and tabs into every input, select and text area of every page and every overlay it opens (the filter panel and sheet, sort, search, the newsletter, the sign-in sheet, the checkout fields and the address sheet with `FILLED=1`) and fails on any outline on the field or its ancestors, on more than one ring that appears around it, and on a field whose look does not change at all. On live 1.0.4 it reports the owner's bug (the price boxes: an outline and 2 rings, the sort select and the newsletter: an outline). Run it with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` from Git Bash (run-checks.sh sets it): otherwise Git Bash turns the page paths into Windows paths.

**A second gap the new check found:** a field in error (`[aria-invalid]`, e.g. the checkout's phone once the contact was saved empty) already wears a 2px ring in the action colour, so focusing it changed nothing. Focused, an error field now takes a 3px ring on the plain ground.

**Dev (14 Nov 2026), the checks a focus change can affect:** ring dark/light/Arabic, underline dark/light, contrast with the bag drawer or phone menu open (light), shapes light/dark (with the field test, the overlays and the filled checkout, 1440/1100/390), shapes teal and Arabic: all **TOTAL 0**.

**Arabic (the "i18n-ar" item).** Not a Layout bug: see "Live pass on 1.0.4"; the live Arabic checks run with `PW=scripts/pw-shop-languages.cjs` and are TOTAL 0. No code change.

**Follow-up (14 Nov 2026):** none parked: the invalid-field focus ring shipped in 1.0.5; next, the shape check's field test on every remaining overlay in the live pass, and the wiki's first page (official-galio.wiki.git does not exist until it is created in a browser).
